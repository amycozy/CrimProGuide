# Mascot Character-Sheet Prompts

Image-generation prompts for building six-pose character sheets like the Felipe
reference for every guide mascot — the five existing characters plus **three new
proposed mascots** for the taxonomy groups that don't have one yet (Pleas/Trial &
Jury, Double Jeopardy & Punishment, Post-Conviction Review).

Every prompt is **self-contained** — paste one block straight into your image tool.
They all share the same *house style* so the whole cast reads as one family.

---

## How to use

1. Pick a character below and paste its full prompt block.
2. Generate at **1:1 square, high resolution** (≈2048px). Append the shared
   **negative prompt** at the end.
3. From the resulting sheet, crop individual poses into the filenames the app
   expects (see the roster). The site uses the **hero** pose for the round
   right-rail guide portrait; `-confused` and `-action` variants map to the
   thinking and action poses (see `character-poses.md`).

> Palette note: keep every character in the muted, earthy, slightly-desaturated
> register of the Felipe reference so they sit together. Badges and small props
> may pick up the guide's process accents — cyan `#0088b0`, magenta `#d6006c`,
> print-gold `#edbb00` — used sparingly.

---

## The canonical six poses

Each sheet is a **2-row × 3-column grid** of the *same* character, full body,
identical design and colors throughout. Reading left-to-right, top row then
bottom:

1. **Hero / default** — standing tall, three-quarter view, confident smile,
   holding the character's **signature tool**, doctrine **badge** on the chest.
2. **Thinking** — paw/wing to chin, eyes up, a floating **?**, puzzled.
3. **Skeptical / frustrated** — scratching head, brow furrowed, a floating **!**,
   tool held low.
4. **Alarmed** — startled wide eyes, bristled fur, motion lines, reacting to a
   small yellow **warning triangle**, pointing.
5. **Action** — actively doing the character's job with **role-specific gear**,
   plus a **secondary prop** on the ground.
6. **In costume / resolved** — wearing a **themed outfit**, holding a
   **document/prop**, pleased expression, badge visible.

## Shared negative prompt (append to every generation)

```
Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, harsh gradients, neon or saturated colors,
extra limbs, malformed hands/paws, inconsistent character design between poses,
cluttered background, watermark, gibberish or misspelled text.
```

# Existing mascots

## Felipe the Fourth Amendment Fox — `felipe.png`
*Groups 01–02 · privacy, searches & seizures.*

```
Character model sheet, six poses of ONE character in a 2x3 grid. Vintage 1950s
storybook cartoon style: clean thick near-black ink outlines of even weight,
soft flat cel shading, muted earthy palette, fine paper-grain texture, warm
cream background (#f4ecd6), each figure casting a soft oval drop shadow. Keep the
design, colors and proportions identical in all six poses. CHARACTER: Felipe, a
friendly anthropomorphic fox with muted forest-olive-green fur, darker green
ears, mane and bushy tail, cream muzzle and belly, small black nose, large
expressive eyes; standing on two legs, standard cartoon proportions. POSES,
top row then bottom row: (1) HERO — standing tall in three-quarter view,
confident smile, holding a round magnifying glass up in one paw, a gold shield
badge reading "4th" on his chest; (2) THINKING — paw to chin, eyes glancing up,
a floating question mark, puzzled; (3) SKEPTICAL — scratching the back of his
head, brow furrowed, a floating exclamation mark, magnifier held low in the
other paw; (4) ALARMED — startled wide eyes and bristled fur with motion lines,
reacting to a small yellow warning triangle, pointing; (5) ACTION — wearing
brass binocular-goggles and peering intently through the magnifying glass for
hidden evidence, a small brass key resting on the ground beside him;
(6) DETECTIVE — wearing a tan plaid tweed detective coat with the "4th" badge on
the lapel, holding an unfurled warrant scroll, pleased expression. Square 1:1,
high resolution, uncluttered.
```

## Slicing a finished sheet into app assets

For each character, crop three poses to match the existing naming convention
(`character-poses.md`) and drop them in `CrimProGuide/assets/`:

| Pose on the sheet | App file | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (1) Hero | `<name>.png` | right-rail guide portrait, overview gallery |
| (2) Thinking | `<name>-confused.png` | FAQ / common-confusion callouts |
| (5) Action | `<name>-action.png` | role-specific illustrations |

To wire a new mascot into the guide, add it to the `MASCOTS` map and the
relevant group's `mascot` fields in `CrimProGuide/js/main.js` (Jada → group 05,
Deja → group 06, Harlan → group 07).
